# Playbook: Prospect Qualification & Meeting Validation

> **Source Artefact:** Prospect Qualification + Meeting Validation Sheet (Working Execution Artefact)  
> **Scope:** Controlled pre-sales discovery, qualification gates, and commercial routing  
> **Compliance Rule:** Record minimum necessary pre-sales data only; no customer confidential data without approved handling terms.

---

## 1. Pre-Sales Boundary Rule
Free discovery establishes **fit**, clarifies the **presenting problem**, and selects the **commercial route**. 
* **Strictly Prohibited in Discovery:** Do not provide free substantive diagnostic analysis, ranked recommendations, or workflow design during qualification meetings.

---

## 2. Discovery Evidence & Qualification Gates

### Core Discovery Prompts (Scored High / Medium / Low Confidence)
1. **Time Friction:** Where are skilled people losing time?
2. **Outcome:** What business outcome matters most?
3. **Blast Radius:** Which teams, processes, or customers are affected?
4. **Urgency:** Why address this now?
5. **History:** What has already been tried?
6. **Economics:** Is there a genuine budget and decision path?

### Mandatory Pass/Fail Qualification Gates
* Clear business problem and desired outcome.
* Appropriate customer and Waikato / target-market fit.
* Capacity fit (or justified controlled exception).
* Can be scoped without free substantive analysis.
* Commercial route meets margin and pricing standards.
* Risk appears manageable subject to paid discovery / professional validation.
* Decision authority and plausible next step exist.

---

## 3. Commercial Routing Matrix

| Commercial Route | Selection Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Workflow Friction Map (WFM)** | **Default route:** Wider evidence is needed across staff/processes, or the opportunity portfolio remains unclear. |
| **Direct-to-PoV (Exception)** | Only when the workflow is bounded and full Joint Investment Gate evidence is already established (value, scope/owner, feasibility, data readiness, risks, human-in-the-loop rules, decision authority). |
| **AI Strategy Retainer** | A genuine strategic/governance need is established within the provisional service envelope. |
| **AI Training & Adoption** | A defined capability/adoption need is established (pricing/SKU subject to qualification). |
| **No Engagement / Revisit** | Fit, urgency, authority, evidence, economics, or capacity is insufficient. |

---

## 4. Meeting Validation & Founder Sign-Off
Every discovery session captures: (1) propositions immediately understood vs. requiring explanation, (2) high-interest outcomes, (3) objections/risks raised, (4) required evidence to proceed, and (5) perceived value/willingness to pay. All proposals require explicit Founder sign-off (`Proceed` / `Revise` / `Stop`).
