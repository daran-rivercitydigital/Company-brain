## 1. Core Positioning & Value Proposition

* **Mission:** Releasing skilled people to spend more time with customers by identifying and reducing repetitive preparation, reporting, re-keying, and system hand-offs.
* **Core Pillars:** Local (Waikato / Kirikiriroa-Hamilton) • Operationally deep • Security by design • Outcome measured.
* **Target Market:** Waikato professional-services and mid-market organizations (initial learning vertical: accountancy and advisory).
* **Operating Philosophy:** Workflow first and evidence led. Automation and AI are introduced only where evidence supports them, with human approval before consequential actions.

## 2. Five-Stage Delivery Model & Commercial Routes

River City Digital (RCD) moves qualified opportunities from diagnostic evidence to controlled delivery through five stages:

1. **01 — Discover:** Find material workflow friction across core processes.
2. **02 — Prioritise:** Rank opportunities by economic value, feasibility, and risk.
3. **03 — Prove:** Test selected workflows via a paid, bounded, non-production Proof-of-Value (PoV). *(Note: Qualified opportunities with equivalent pre-existing evidence in a Qualification Record may bypass the WFM and proceed directly to PoV).*
4. **04 — Implement:** Deliver fixed-scope solutions governed by formal acceptance gates.
5. **05 — Operate:** Provide ongoing support, monitoring, and reassessment where applicable.

### Separate & Complementary Commercial Routes
* **AI Strategy Retainer:** Ongoing strategic advice, governance, and value review where evidence shows a need (separate scope and approval).
* **AI Training and Adoption:** Staff capability and responsible AI usage training to resolve people/adoption friction (separate scope and approval).
* **PoV Gate Outcomes:** Every PoV concludes with a formal **Proceed / Revise / Stop** decision.

## 3. Workflow Friction Map (WFM) — Flagship Diagnostic

The Workflow Friction Map is RCD's standalone entry diagnostic. It produces an independent decision asset that remains useful even if no downstream implementation occurs.

### Customer Time Commitment & Process

| Stage | Activity | Customer Commitment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01. Kick-off** | Agree scope and nominate survey participants | 30-minute call |
| **02. Survey** | Confidential daily friction survey | 10–15 minutes per nominated person |
| **03. Consolidate** | Group responses and clarify gaps | 10 minutes (if needed) |
| **04. Assess** | Calculate recoverable capacity and rank opportunities | Agreed delivery timeframe (RCD effort) |
| **05. Decision** | Joint Investment Gate to review pack and agree priorities | 60-minute meeting |

### Deliverables (Executive PowerPoint / PDF Pack)
* **Ranked Opportunity Portfolio:** Core workflow opportunities ranked by conservative recoverable annual value, then evaluated for feasibility and risk.
* **Executive Opportunity Cards:** Standardized breakdown of current friction, annual value, evidence quality, feasibility, risk, complexity, and indicative ROI.
* **Clear Action Recommendations:** Each shortlisted opportunity is assigned a disposition of **Progress**, **Consider**, or **Defer** (scoring weights remain provisional through the Founding Partner cohort).
* **Lightweight Backlog & Investment Gate Material:** Ready for executive decision-making with zero obligation to proceed to build.

### Scope Boundaries
* **In Scope:** Identifying economically material friction, grouping staff evidence into shared workflows, testing root causes (process, data, definitions, hand-offs, or training—not just technology), and producing an executive decision pack.
* **Out of Scope:** Documenting every organizational activity, treating every suggestion as an investment case, assuming recovered time equals automatic cash savings, guaranteeing ROI/feasibility, or locking the customer into implementation.

## 5. Security, Privacy & Evidence Governance Commitments

*Design commitments are aligned with the NZ Privacy Act 2020, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, and SOC 2, subject to technical validation and qualified NZ privacy review (no formal certification or attestation is claimed).*

* **Confidential Input & Non-Attributed Reporting:** Survey inputs remain identifiable to RCD solely for targeted clarification; customer deliverables describe workflows and functions without naming individual staff.
* **Isolated Processing & Limited Retention:** Raw assessment evidence is access-controlled and deleted when no longer required—**no later than 10 business days after acceptance**, subject to documented lawful exceptions.
* **Public AI Training Exclusion:** Customer evidence is used only with approved commercial AI services and configured controls; never used to train public AI models.
* **Human Accountability:** Deterministic rules perform calculations; AI may assist with grouping or drafting, but RCD approves all assumptions, rankings, risks, and recommendations, and humans approve consequential outputs.

## 6. Standard Pre-Sales FAQ Guardrails

1. **Cost & Time:** WFM starts at $1,500 + GST based on invited headcount. Customer effort is limited to a 30-minute kick-off, 10–15 minute staff surveys, optional 10-minute clarifications, and a 60-minute decision gate.
2. **Lock-In:** Zero lock-in. The WFM stands alone; PoV, implementation, and operations each require separate scope and approval.
3. **Government Co-Funding:** Potential government co-funding may exist for eligible AI implementation programmes, but RCD never assumes eligibility, never builds pricing around expected funding, and treats standalone training as non-fundable unless part of an approved adoption programme.
4. **Evidence Protection:** Non-attributed reporting, isolated processing, no public AI training, and hard deletion of raw evidence within 10 business days post-acceptance.
5. **Prioritisation Logic:** Driven by economic materiality ($\text{Value} \times \text{Feasibility}$, adjusted for risk) using deterministic calculations validated by RCD human judgment.
6. **Root-Cause Analysis:** Uses iterative "Why" questioning and cycle verification to test whether friction stems from unclear definitions, inconsistent input formats, or process gaps before recommending technology.
