Services Agreement Baseline v9.0
Purpose
This document defines the baseline commercial and contractual framework for River City Digital (RCD) service engagements.

This baseline supports the V9 business architecture and AI Adoption operating model.

The objective of all service engagements is to identify, validate, deploy and operate practical AI solutions that create measurable business value.

Workflow analysis, process improvement and technology reviews remain supporting activities rather than primary commercial outcomes.

Engagement Structure
This agreement does not authorise work by itself.

Every engagement requires an Engagement Schedule.

Each Engagement Schedule must identify:

Engagement type
Deliverables
Fee structure
Customer responsibilities
Timeline
Acceptance criteria
Approved technology platforms
Where a conflict exists, the Engagement Schedule prevails for that engagement.

Fees and Payment
Unless otherwise stated:

Fees are stated in New Zealand Dollars.
GST is additional where applicable.
Fixed-fee engagements require payment terms defined in the Engagement Schedule.
Subscription services are invoiced in advance unless otherwise agreed.
Approved third-party licensing and consumption charges are additional unless stated otherwise.
Customer Responsibilities
The customer remains responsible for:

Business decisions
Approval of recommendations
Approval of AI-generated outputs
User adoption
Operational decisions
The customer must provide accurate information reasonably required to deliver the services.

Privacy and Data Protection
RCD will:

Minimise customer data collection
Apply least-privilege access principles
Maintain logical separation between customers
Use customer data only for authorised purposes
Support applicable New Zealand privacy obligations
Approved platforms and processing locations must be identified within the Engagement Schedule.

Artificial Intelligence Use
RCD may utilise:

Approved AI platforms
Approved automation services
Deterministic business rules
Internal frameworks and tooling
AI outputs may contain inaccuracies.

Human review remains essential.

Neither party may rely solely upon AI-generated outputs for consequential decisions without appropriate review.

Intellectual Property
The customer retains ownership of:

Customer data
Customer materials
Customer confidential information
RCD retains ownership of:

Methods
Frameworks
Templates
Scoring models
Internal tooling
Background intellectual property
Customer-specific deliverables may be used internally by the customer after payment.

No ownership of RCD intellectual property transfers unless explicitly agreed in writing.

Productisation Rights
RCD may develop reusable products, frameworks, components and services based on lessons learned across engagements.

Any such reuse must not disclose customer confidential information.

Customer-specific data remains customer-owned.

Reusable platform intellectual property remains owned by RCD unless otherwise agreed.

Confidentiality
Each party must protect confidential information received from the other.

Confidential information may only be disclosed:

To authorised personnel
To professional advisers
Where required by law
Equivalent confidentiality obligations must apply to any subcontractor granted access.

Liability
RCD does not warrant:

Specific savings
Specific ROI outcomes
Regulatory compliance
Error-free operation
Continuous availability of third-party services
Liability limitations, exclusions, and insurance requirements are governed by the executed Engagement Schedule and applicable law.

Strategic Principle
Workflow analysis is the discovery mechanism.

AI adoption is the outcome.

Managed AI Services are the preferred long-term customer relationship.

Productisation is the preferred scaling mechanism.
