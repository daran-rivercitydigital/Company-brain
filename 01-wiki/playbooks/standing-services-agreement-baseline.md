# Playbook: Standing Services Agreement Commercial Baseline

> **Source Artefact:** RCD Services Agreement Draft v0.2 (2026-11-09)  
> **Status:** Commercial working draft — requires qualified New Zealand commercial & privacy legal validation before signature  
> **Governing Law:** New Zealand (Auckland Business Days)

---

## 1. Contract Architecture & Precedence
* **Master + Schedule Structure:** Standing terms (`RCD-MSA-[nnnn]`) authorise zero work alone; each engagement requires a signed **Engagement Schedule** (WFM, PoV, Implementation, Managed Service, Retainer, or Training). No follow-on stage is automatic or exclusive.
* **Order of Precedence:** Engagement Schedule (for that engagement only, where varied clauses are expressly named) -> Master Services Agreement -> Referenced supporting documents.
* **Contracting Entity:** Supports sole trader (`Daran Stokes trading as River City Digital`) for initial pilot work, with fresh re-papering under `River City Digital Limited` upon incorporation.

---

## 2. Core Commercial & Payment Standards
* **Currency & Tax:** All fees in **NZD**, excluding GST (GST charged only once registration is confirmed).
* **Standard Payment Terms:** **50% upfront** before capacity is reserved or delivery starts; **50% on Accepted Delivery** for engagements <= 30 calendar days. Invoices payable within **14 calendar days**.
* **Overdue Suspension:** Undisputed invoices > 7 days overdue allow suspension of Services after written notice (interest/collection mechanics pending legal review).
* **Change Control:** Any material change to scope, participants, assumptions, dates, or limits (e.g., extra interviews or Opportunity Cards) requires a bilaterally signed written **Change Request**.

---

## 3. Delivery, Acceptance & Rescheduling
* **Acceptance Mechanics (Clause 7):** Delivery starts on the **Assessment Ready Date**. Once RCD notifies readiness, the customer has a **10 Business Day Review Period** to accept or give written notice of **Material Non-Conformance** (disagreement with professional judgement or rankings is not non-conformance). Silence deems acceptance on Day 10.
* **Gate Independence:** A Joint Investment Gate / Executive Review Workshop or data close-out confirmation does not delay or condition Accepted Delivery unless the Schedule states otherwise.
* **Rescheduling Baseline (Subject to Legal Review):** Free with >= 5 Business Days' notice; 50% fee within 5 Business Days; 100% fee within 2 Business Days.

---

## 4. Privacy, AI Governance & IP Protection
* **Data Minimisation & Isolation:** Customer Data is segregated per engagement under least-privilege access. **Zero Customer Data** may be placed into public consumer AI services or used to train public models.
* **Survey Anonymity:** Responses remain identifiable to RCD during active analysis for clarification, but Deliverables never attribute comments to individuals and respondent-level data is never handed to customer leadership.
* **Human Accountability:** Deterministic rules and approved commercial AI may assist drafting/grouping, but RCD approves all rankings and recommendations; the customer retains sole responsibility for business/investment decisions.
* **Intellectual Property:** RCD retains 100% ownership of **RCD Background IP** (methods, question banks, scoring logic, workbooks, templates). Upon full payment, customers receive an internal business-use licence to Deliverables and may share them under confidentiality with **Permitted Recipients** (accountants, lawyers, implementation partners) who receive zero licence to RCD Background IP.
* **Liability & Insurance:** Working baseline caps aggregate liability at 12 months' fees (or Contracted Fee for single WFM; 2x cap modelled for privacy/security carve-outs—pending NZ legal review). Schedules explicitly disclose whether PI/Cyber insurance is held at the Effective Date.
