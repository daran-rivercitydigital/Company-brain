## 1. Cohort position

Client-WKO-01 is the Pilot Customer. It is not Customer 1 and not a Founding Partner. It does not count toward the Customer-3 repeatability gate, the demand test, the effort-reduction baseline, reuse, cohort margin or managed-effort measures.

* No Founding Partner discount.
* Price is bespoke, set by effort and value, and does not set or change any WFM tier price.
* No margin floor applies. The value to RCD is validation of the survey, scoring, Executive Opportunity Card and pack, plus the first real effort evidence.
* The engagement occupies one of the two normal concurrent paid-engagement slots while active.
* Record hours, direct costs and lessons separately. They may inform WFM assets and the Customer 1 proposal. They are not cohort evidence.
* Review every Pilot exception at engagement close and before the Customer 1 proposal. None carries into a Founding Partner agreement by default.
* The no-margin-floor exception was approved by the founder on 19 November 2026 and expires at Pilot close.

* The Pilot must use the approved WFM environment in the [technology stack](../architecture/technology-stack-requirements.md).
